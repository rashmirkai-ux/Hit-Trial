## Brand & Style

The design system is crafted for high-stakes urban mobility, AI-assisted pathfinding, and personal safety navigation. The interface projects radical clarity, immediate trust, and composed reliability under cognitive load. Users range from late-night commuters and pedestrians seeking safe routes to emergency responders and travelers in unfamiliar environments. The emotional posture must never cause panic; it delivers calm, decisive awareness.

The design movement combines **Modern High-Clarity Corporate** and **Functional Ambient Tactility**:
- Uncluttered, generous whitespaces that allow cognitive prioritization during movement.
- High visual legibility across extreme outdoor ambient light conditions.
- Functional semantic signaling where color instantly conveys safe zones, alert thresholds, and critical action triggers without ambiguity.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around high WCAG AAA contrast ratios and clear semantic tiering:

- **Primary Interactive (`#2563EB`, active `#1D4ED8`)**: Used for core navigation paths, active tracking pins, interactive CTAs, and confirmed system confirmations.
- **Safety & Verification (`#059669`, accent `#10B981`)**: Signals well-lit streets, verified safe zones, crowd-monitored routes, and active patrol zones.
- **Hazard & Caution (`#D97706`)**: Reserved for construction reroutes, poorly-lit paths, incident advisories, and cautious routing suggestions.
- **Critical & SOS (`#DC2626`)**: Strictly allocated for panic triggers, emergency live sharing, immediate threats, and emergency stop actions. Never diluted on secondary elements.
- **Surfaces & Backgrounds**: Crisp pure white (`#FFFFFF`) for elevated action cards and interactive HUD modals, layered over soft base canvas slate tones (`#F8FAFC`, `#F1F5F9`).
- **Typography & Text Contrast**: Deepest midnight slate (`#0F172A`) for all primary headings, titles, and critical route notices, descending to `#1E293B` for standard body copy, and neutral slate (`#475569`, `#64748B`) for secondary metadata and timestamps.
- **Subtle Structure**: Borders rely exclusively on crisp, low-glare slate outlines (`#E2E8F0`).

In Dark Mode, surfaces flip to `#090D16` and `#0F172A`, borders adjust to `#1E293B`, and text tokens invert to `#F8FAFC` and `#94A3B8` without lowering interactive contrast.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy is optimized for instantaneous legibility in both hands-free mounting conditions and direct handheld glanceability:

- **Display & Headings (Plus Jakarta Sans)**: Friendly yet commanding geometry. Large aperture shapes prevent letter blending when reading at arms-length or during physical transit.
- **Body & Informational Readouts (Inter)**: Tall x-height and standardized optical metrics prevent visual fatigue across safety incident descriptions, step-by-step navigation, and telemetry data.
- **Rules**:
  - No text element is rendered smaller than 11px (`label-sm`), strictly bounded to status tags and badges.
  - Sentence case is mandatory across all user prompts, safety alerts, and navigation instructions to prevent urgent alert fatigue.
  - Generous line heights are strictly enforced to avoid line collision when reading under vibration or while walking.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout system balances standard mobile application ergonomics with high-density navigational split-views:

- **Grid Strategy**:
  - **Mobile (< 768px)**: Fluid single-column layout with safe area edge padding of `margin` (16px). Persistent floating bottom action sheet and high-visibility bottom navigation bar.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8-column layout with 24px margins; supports map split-screen alongside list-based turn-by-turn guidance.
  - **Desktop (>= 1024px)**: 12-column layout with 40px margins; side-rail situational awareness feed (4 columns) paired with map canvas (8 columns).
- **Spatial Rhythm**:
  - Built on a firm 4px/8px modular spacing baseline.
  - Interactive tap elements adhere strictly to minimum hit targets of 44px x 44px (48px preferred for critical triggers).
  - Floating interface components maintain at least `space-md` clearance from screen perimeters and dynamic system hardware cutouts.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy employs a multi-tiered surface layering model using soft ambient diffusion rather than dark, harsh drop shadows:

- **Base Layer (Level 0)**: Viewport canvas (`#F8FAFC`). Flat background serving as the foundational terrain or basemap canvas.
- **Card Tier (Level 1)**: Pure white `#FFFFFF` surface bordered by a crisp `1px solid #E2E8F0` with a subtle contact shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.06)`. Used for non-floating list items, settings rows, and static metric displays.
- **Floating Controls Tier (Level 2)**: Persistent bottom sheets, rerouting snackbars, and interactive search bars: `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04), 0 4px 6px -2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.02)` bordered by `1px solid #E2E8F0`.
- **Urgent HUD & Dialogs (Level 3)**: SOS prompts, active path hazard alerts, and emergency contact modals: elevated with `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04)` over a semi-transparent scrim (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.4)`).

## Shapes

The design system maintains a welcoming, humane, and protective aesthetic through cohesive rounded curvature:

- **Containers & Cards**: Styled with `rounded-xl` (12px / 0.75rem to 1rem) to soften technical map overlays and create approachable containment boundaries.
- **Interactive Controls**: Inputs, standard buttons, and action sheets adopt `rounded-lg` (8px to 12px) for structured finger placement.
- **Status Badges & Quick Action Chips**: Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`) geometry to instantly separate metadata indicators from tappable rectangular containers.
- **Critical Action Triggers (SOS)**: Full circle geometry on mobile triggers to facilitate single-hand emergency activation while preventing accidental misclicks.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid Royal Blue (`#2563EB`), text `#FFFFFF`, minimum height 48px, horizontal padding `space-lg`, border-radius 12px. Pressed state: `#1D4ED8`. Focus state: outer ring 2px offset `#2563EB`.
- **Secondary / Ghost**: Background transparent or `#F1F5F9`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, text `#1E293B`.
- **SOS Button**: High-visibility Crimson (`#DC2626`), white text, bold label. Includes a mandatory continuous 2-second hold-to-confirm progress ring to prevent unintended triggers.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- Minimum touch field height: 48px.
- Background `#FFFFFF` with `1px solid #E2E8F0`, transitioning smoothly to `border-color: #2563EB` and a subtle 3px ring (`rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.15)`) on focus.
- Prefix search/navigation icon placed left; dynamic route-clearing and microphone action icons placed right with minimum 44px tap targets.

### Safety Status Chips & Badges
- **Safe Zone Badge**: Emerald tint background (`#ECFDF5`), border `#A7F3D0`, text `#065F46`, paired with a solid green status dot.
- **Caution Advisory Badge**: Amber tint background (`#FFFBEB`), border `#FDE68A`, text `#92400E`.
- **Incident Chip**: High-contrast Crimson tint background (`#FEF2F2`), border `#FECACA`, text `#991B1B`.

### Lists & Navigation Cards
- Contained within `#FFFFFF` cards, structured with horizontal dividers (`#F1F5F9`).
- Primary information (street, ETA, route type) rendered in `headline-sm` or `label-lg`, secondary distance details in `body-sm` (`#64748B`).
- Always provides a right-aligned clear chevron or route direction indicator icon.

### Form Checkboxes & Radio Toggles
- Sizing fixed at 20px x 20px enclosed within a 44px invisible tap container.
- Selected state fills with `#2563EB`, displaying a crisp white indicator. Border in resting state: `1.5px solid #CBD5E1`.

### Navigation Floating Action HUD
- Floating safe-navigation panel pinned to the viewport bottom.
- Houses real-time AI route assessment, quick re-route trigger, and safe-checkpoint ping button. Uses Level 2 elevation with a 1px slate border.
